# Task C1: Apply Design Thinking

## C1.1 Empathize (Questions for Bank Customers)
1. What key information or assurances do you look for before deciding to check loan eligibility on a mobile app?
2. Have you ever started checking loan eligibility online and stopped midway? If so, what made you stop?
3. If a loan application is not eligible right now, what feedback would help you understand why and what to do next?

## C1.2 Define (Problem Statement)
How might we provide Meridian Bank customers with a simple and clear way to check loan eligibility on their mobile app so they know how much they can borrow without filling out long forms?

## C1.3 Ideate (Solution Approaches)
1. **Interactive Loan Calculator**: An in-app slider tool where users adjust income, existing EMIs, and loan amount to see real-time eligibility updates and maximum loan limits.
2. **Pre-Approved Quick Assessment**: For logged-in customers, estimate eligibility automatically using existing bank account history so users can see pre-approved limits in one tap.
